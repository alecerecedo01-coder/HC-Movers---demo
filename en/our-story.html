Our story

25+ years moving industry.

HC Movers was founded in Monterrey in 2023 by partners with more than 25 years in specialized transport, heavy lifting and rigging.

Who we are

Heavy
Cargo Movers.

One responsible party, start to finish.

HC Movers S.A.P.I. de C.V. is a team of professionals that brings engineering, multimodal specialized transport, heavy lifting, rigging and installation of very heavy or very large components into a single service. We work with commercial and operating partners who contribute specialized equipment and crews.

  • More safety and quality on every project.
  • Less cost, time and risk.
  • National and international projects.
