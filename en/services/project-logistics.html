02 · We move

Project logistics: one party responsible from origin to destination.

We coordinate transport, rigging, storage, permits and customs so your equipment arrives complete, on time and ready to install.

Quote this service Talk to an engineer
Reference image
The challenge

A project is not a shipment.

A production line may arrive in dozens of pieces, from several countries, by ship, rail and road. If each leg is handled by someone else, nobody owns the handoffs. We bring every leg under one plan and one responsible party.

What it includes

  • End-to-end project planning
  • Project and break-bulk cargo
  • Multimodal transport
  • Customs coordination
  • Temporary storage
  • Door-to-door to the foundation

Project Cargo

Planning and execution for special, heavy or oversized cargo that belongs to a project.

02

Project logistics

Full coordination of transport, rigging, storage, permits and every other activity the project needs.

  • Master move schedule
  • One point of contact for the whole project
  • Progress reporting

03

Multimodal transport

A combination of sea, rail and road transport to take the cargo from origin to destination.

  • Port, rail and road in one plan
  • Transfers and intermediate handling
  • Cost and time optimization

04

Customs

Management and coordination of imports and exports for special projects.

  • Coordination with customs brokers
  • Documentation for project cargo
  • Clearance at port and border

05

Door-to-Door

We coordinate the full trip, from the supplier’s facility to the customer’s final site.

  • Pickup at the origin plant
  • Every leg under one plan
  • Delivered on site, ready for rigging
