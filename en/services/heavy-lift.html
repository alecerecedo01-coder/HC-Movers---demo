03 · We lift

Heavy Lift: heavy lifts done by plan, not by luck.

Lifting and placing heavy equipment with specialized cranes, in single or tandem lifts, always with a written lift plan.

Quote this service Talk to an engineer
The challenge

Lifting is not the hard part. Placing is.

A heavy lift depends on the right crane, the ground it stands on, the rigging and the coordination between operator and signal person. We calculate every lift and run it with on-site supervision so the piece lands where it should.

What it includes

  • Lift study and crane selection
  • Tandem lifts
  • Rigging gear sized to the load
  • Ground and outrigger review
  • Signal person and on-site supervision
  • Upending and positioning
