03 · We lift

Segment launching and erection.

Complete solutions for erecting rail viaducts, bridges and segmental structures with launching, lifting and placement of precast segments.

Quote this service Talk to an engineer
Reference image · 42 Walkers, CC0
The challenge

Equipment, engineering and operation under one contract.

A segmental viaduct is built span by span: the launching gantry picks each segment, holds it in place and moves on to the next span. The challenge is not just having the equipment but mobilizing, assembling, operating and feeding it with segments without stopping the work front. HC Movers integrates all of it.

What it includes

  • Segment Launching Gantry rental
  • Erection engineering and method statements
  • Equipment mobilization
  • Assembly and commissioning
  • Specialized operators
  • Segment lifting and handling
  • Segment logistics
  • Rigging
  • Heavy Haul
  • Dismantling and demobilization
Technology proven in Mexico

Launching gantry · precast segments.

Gantry erection has already been used in Mexico on complex rail projects. On the Mexico City–Toluca Interurban Train, the elevated viaduct was built span by span with precast segments and launching gantries.

3.5 kmof elevated rail viaduct
78spans with a self-launching gantry
2spans per week on one front
≈26segments placed at that pace
Public reference for the technology (Rizzani de Eccher, VSL and the Mexico City government). HC Movers did not take part in that project; we cite it as technical precedent in Mexico.

Erection engineering & method
