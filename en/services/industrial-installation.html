04 · We install

Industrial installation: all the way to final position.

Installation, removal, final setting and decommissioning of machinery, equipment and structures inside industrial plants.

Quote this service Talk to an engineer
The challenge

Moving is pointless if the equipment is not ready.

The job ends when the equipment sits on its foundation, aligned and ready to hook up, or when the old plant is cleared. We dismantle, pack, move and reinstall with the same team from start to finish.

What it includes

  • Machinery and structure installation
  • Dismantling and packing
  • On-site setting and leveling
  • Equipment decommissioning
  • Plant relocation
  • Removal of out-of-service equipment
